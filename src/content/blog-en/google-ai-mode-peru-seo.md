In September 2025, Google launched **AI Mode in Spanish for Latin America, including Peru**. It is available on the results page and in the Google app for Android and iOS. For brands it means one thing: more and more searches are answered with an AI-generated text that cites a few pages, instead of the classic list of ten links.

## What AI Mode is and how it works

According to Google, AI Mode uses a custom version of **Gemini 2.5** and has three key features:

- **It handles long, complex questions**, such as comparing options or planning something step by step.
- **It breaks the query into subtopics** and runs several searches at once (what Google calls *query fan-out*). It then combines the results into a single answer with links.
- **It is multimodal**: you can ask with text, voice or images.

Google also clarifies that it only shows a generated answer when it has enough confidence; otherwise it shows traditional results.

## What does not change

Hema Budaraju, Vice President of Product for Google Search, summed it up at the launch: *“The foundation of what good content is has not changed.”* AI Mode relies on the same quality and ranking systems as the search engine. A slow website with generic content that is hard to crawl will not show up here either.

## What does change

Because the AI breaks each question into several searches, content that clearly answers **specific sub-questions** competes better. A page that says “we are the best at marketing” answers nothing; one that explains “how much to invest in Google Ads for an online store in Lima, with examples” does.

## How to adapt your SEO, step by step

1. **Answer real questions.** Look at what your customers ask on WhatsApp or in meetings and turn it into content. Each frequent question can be a section with its own heading.
2. **Get to the point at the start of each section.** A direct answer in the first lines and the detail afterwards. That is what the AI can easily cite.
3. **Bring your own experience.** Cases, figures from your projects, mistakes you have seen. That is what a model cannot invent and what sets your content apart.
4. **Structure the page well.** Clear headings, lists, tables and structured data (schema) so the search engine understands what each part is about.
5. **Take care of the technical side.** Speed, mobile version, pages that can be crawled and indexed, and an up-to-date sitemap.
6. **Strengthen your brand beyond your website.** Media mentions, industry directories, reviews and complete profiles (such as Google Business Profile) help the AI recognize you as a trustworthy source.

## How to measure whether you appear

There is currently no separate AI Mode report in every tool, so it is best to combine signals:

- In **Google Search Console**, check which long, question-style queries bring you impressions.
- In **GA4**, watch the organic traffic of the pages that answer questions and their conversion rate.
- Run periodic manual searches in AI Mode with the key questions in your industry and note whether it cites you.

## In summary

AI Mode does not replace SEO: it makes it more demanding. Useful, specific content with real experience, published on a fast and well-structured website, wins. That is exactly what has always worked, now with less room for filler content.
