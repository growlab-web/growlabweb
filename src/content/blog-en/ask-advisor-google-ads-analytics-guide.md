Google wants you to stop jumping between tabs to understand your campaigns. Its answer is **Ask Advisor**, a Gemini-based AI agent that lives inside Google Ads and Google Analytics and that you can ask, in plain language, what happened to your sales, why costs went up or what you should change.

It is still in beta and not every account can see it, but it is worth understanding now what it does, what it doesn’t do and how to use it without giving up control of your campaigns. Here is a summary based on Google’s official documentation.

## What Ask Advisor is

Google introduced it on **May 20, 2026** as a “cross-product” agent that brings your marketing tools together in one experience. Google Ads, Google Analytics and Google Marketing Platform already had their own agents, and Ask Advisor coordinates them; Google announced that a **Merchant Center** agent will join later.

The idea is that you can ask for something like “find new customers for my products” and the agent will use your product information and your data to propose a campaign, explain what worked and suggest next steps.

On **August 10, 2026**, Google added new features:

- **AI summaries on the Analytics homepage**, showing what changed since your last visit (sales peaks, traffic shifts), with the option to get alerts by email or phone.
- **AI insight cards on the Google Ads homepage**, with a prompt box to request a custom analysis.
- **Dashboards built from text** in Google Ads, with a summary that explains the “why” behind the data; Google says they are coming soon to Analytics.
- **Benchmarking in Analytics**: your results compared with anonymized averages from similar businesses.

## What it can do in each tool

| | Google Ads | Google Analytics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reports | Generates reports and explains performance changes | Answers general and detailed questions about metrics, dimensions and events |
| Diagnosis | Helps with performance, policy, billing and identity verification issues | Explains the “why” behind performance changes |
| Recommendations | Suggests optimizations, ad text, images, keywords and campaign ideas | Suggests optimizations and next steps |
| Actions | Can change individual campaign settings **with your approval** | Gives step-by-step guidance on features and looks up setup details (measurement ID, data streams) |

You open it from the Ask Advisor icon in the top-right corner of the account or by typing your question in the search bar. In Analytics you can also launch it from chart cards, which offer suggested questions.

## Can I use it in my account yet?

It depends, and this is the key detail for businesses outside English-speaking markets:

- It is a **beta** and only appears if your account is eligible.
- Google says the beta is available for **English-language accounts**. For Analytics, the official help page specifies that it works in **properties with English as the selected language**, and that support for other languages is planned.
- In Google Ads it is **not available in manager (MCC) accounts**: you need to open the advertiser account itself.

If you don’t see the icon, it is not a setup mistake: it simply hasn’t reached your account yet.

## What happens to your data

This is the question we hear most. According to Google Analytics Help:

- Prompts and answers are **not used to train, fine-tune or evaluate AI models**.
- Chat history is stored for **55 days** and then deleted.
- Answers about your data come **only from the property** you have open; data is not mixed across properties.

The usual rule still applies: Google Analytics **prohibits collecting personally identifiable information** (names, emails, phone numbers), so don’t send it in your events.

## How to use it while staying in control

Google warns that Ask Advisor **may have limitations and make mistakes**, and that you keep control over what it generates. These practices help you get value from it safely:

1. **Fix your measurement first.** An agent analyzing incomplete or duplicated data will give you wrong conclusions with great confidence. Make sure your conversions are set up correctly; if in doubt, read our guide on [attribution windows in GA4, Meta and Google Ads](/en/blog/attribution-windows-ga4-meta-google-ads).
2. **Ask specific questions.** “Why did conversions from the search campaign drop last week compared with the week before?” works better than “how am I doing?”
3. **Check the numbers in the original report.** Treat the answer as a lead and confirm it in the standard report before deciding on budgets.
4. **Approve changes one by one.** Being able to change campaigns with your approval doesn’t mean you should accept everything: review budgets, bids and targeting.
5. **Review suggested text and images** against your brand voice and advertising policies before publishing them.
6. **Check critical items in settings.** Google says billing status, identity verification and appeal outcomes should be reviewed directly in the account settings.

## In summary

Ask Advisor turns Google Ads and Analytics into something you can “ask”: it explains changes, suggests improvements and, with your approval, applies adjustments. For now it is a beta for English-language accounts, so not everyone will see it yet. When it reaches your account, use it as a fast analyst, not as an autopilot: with well-measured data, clear questions and a review of every change before you accept it.
