On October 6, 2026, at Advertising Week New York, Meta presented a batch of artificial intelligence features for advertisers on Facebook and Instagram. Some are already available to everyone; others are still being tested or will arrive “in the coming months.” Telling them apart is what will save you the most time as you plan your year-end campaigns.

In this article we sum up what Meta announced, the status of each update and how to make the most of the features you can switch on in your account today.

## Available to everyone now

### Customer lifecycle strategy

According to Meta, this feature is **available to all advertisers**. It lets you label a campaign as **acquisition** (winning new customers) and get recommendations on **which existing customers to exclude**. It is useful if your budget is being spent showing “first purchase” ads to people who already bought from you. Meta is also testing the ability to exclude an entire group of existing customers with a single audience tag.

### Videos from images with Advantage+ creative

Generating **video from your static images** inside Advantage+ creative is now **generally available**. Meta backs its focus on video with its own figure: more than **60% of the time** people spend on Facebook and Instagram goes to watching video. If your brand only has product photos, this is the fastest way to test motion formats without producing from scratch.

## Expanding or in testing

### AI assistant in Ads Manager

The Meta AI business assistant helps you plan, set up, optimize and measure campaigns. Meta says advertisers who adopt its recommendations **often see 5% more conversions at the same cost per conversion** (a figure from Meta itself, not a guaranteed result). What’s new:

- **More memory and context:** it remembers previous conversations, last quarter’s tests, seasonality and the goals you gave it.
- **Creative insights:** it brings in Ads Creative Studio data so your ads’ performance shapes your strategy.
- **In testing:** importing your strategy, tone of voice and brand guidelines from other AI tools.
- **Agentic capabilities (in testing):** creating campaigns, generating images and copy, updating targeting and changing budgets through conversation, with previews and approvals so you decide what to delegate.
- **Scheduled tasks and alerts (in testing):** weekly summaries, performance monitoring and reminders to review budgets.

### Ads Creative Studio

This is the creative workspace Meta launched earlier this year for a small group of partners, and it is now **expanding to more advertisers**. It shows which creatives are working and why, and lets you generate new video concepts and variations, editing the hook, audio, clip length, text, captions and end scene.

### Product videos from your catalog

For catalog advertisers, Meta has a **beta** that turns product images into videos at scale, through Advantage+ video generation in Ads Manager and the product video creator in Commerce Manager. Only eligible advertisers can see it.

## Coming later

| Update | What it does | Status according to Meta |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI audience discovery | You describe your ideal customer in your own words and it suggests audiences in detailed targeting | By the end of 2026 |
| Product recommendations | Analyzes the ad’s image, text and landing page to suggest products that pair well | In the coming months |
| Meta Ads MCP server | Lets you manage campaigns from other AI tools; agent work arrives as a draft and needs approval | Aimed at large advertisers and agencies |
| Shopify checkout in Messenger | Buy products from Shopify stores without leaving the chat with the business agent | Announced as a partnership, no date |

## How to use it in your campaigns

1. **Separate acquisition from retention.** Turn on the customer lifecycle strategy and exclude existing customers from campaigns meant to attract new ones.
2. **Test AI-generated video** from your best images as a variation within the same ad set, and compare results before scaling.
3. **Review every proposal before approving it.** The assistant and agents ask for confirmation: use it to check budget, audiences and copy.
4. **Look after your conversion data.** All of Meta’s AI learns from the signals you send; if your pixel and Conversions API are not set up properly, its recommendations will be worse. We explain it in [Meta Pixel and Conversions API](/en/blog/meta-pixel-conversions-api).
5. **Measure outside Meta too.** Compare results with GA4 and your real sales, keeping in mind each platform’s [attribution windows](/en/blog/attribution-windows-ga4-meta-google-ads).

## In summary

Meta is betting on AI to help you plan, create and optimize campaigns, but what you can use today without waiting is the customer lifecycle strategy and video generation from images in Advantage+ creative. The rest (agents, scheduled tasks, audiences described in your own words) is in testing or still to come. Use what is available, stay in control of every change and make sure your measurement data is reliable.
